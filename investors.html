Building the operating layer for aviation ground operations.

ThruFly is a mobile-first platform for ramp crews and the leaders who depend on them. This page is a short, plain overview for operators and investors who want context.

The problem

Ramp work runs on radio, paper and memory.

Turnarounds involve many crews, equipment and hand-offs. Leaders often see what happened only after the aircraft has left, and the record of who did what is hard to reconstruct.

What we are building

Frontline-first software.

Digital turnaround procedures for crews, equipment tracking, a timestamped record and visibility for station leadership. Designed to work offline and to run on its own, without an integration project.

Why now

Pressure on safety, compliance and turn performance.

Operators are asked for more documentation and more visibility from the apron, while crews already carry mobile devices. Frontline tools that capture the record as the work happens are a natural next step.

Go-to-market

Founder-led and pilot-first.

Start with one station for 60 to 90 days and agreed metrics, show the result, then expand. Airports, airlines, ground handlers and FBOs each get a role-specific path.

Where we are

Early, and candid about it.

  • ThruFly LLC is a Florida company, formed in February 2026
  • The MVP is founder-built and self-funded to date
  • A first station pilot is in planning; we have no customers or revenue yet
  • Federal contracting registration (SAM.gov) is in progress
